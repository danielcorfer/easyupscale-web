In one sentence

EasyUpscale AI enlarges and enhances photos with AI on your own Windows PC: no cloud, no subscription, no queue.

Short description (about 50 words)

EasyUpscale AI is a Windows app that enlarges and enhances photos with artificial intelligence on your own graphics card. Nothing is uploaded. Auto mode picks the model by itself, Advanced mode gives full control, and a before-and-after slider shows what changed. It is free (up to 2×, no watermark); a one-time license adds Advanced mode, 4× enlarging, batches, the watched folder and extra formats.

Long description

Most AI upscalers are websites: you upload a photo, wait in a queue and pay per picture or per month. EasyUpscale AI works the other way round. The AI runs on your own graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, through DirectX 12), so your pictures never leave your computer, there is no file size limit set by a service and no subscription.

In Auto mode the app looks at the picture and picks the model, the enlargement and the look. Advanced mode (with the license) lets you choose from six models, including our own text model, blend two of them, set the output size (2×, 4×, 4K, Full HD) and fine-tune sharpness and noise. A live before-and-after slider shows exactly what changed. With the license, whole folders can be enlarged in a batch, and a watched folder enlarges new pictures in the background. The app saves PNG and JPEG for free, plus WebP, AVIF, TIFF and BMP with the license, and keeps transparency and metadata.

The free version enlarges up to 2× with no watermark. A one-time license of €14.99 (website or Microsoft Store) unlocks Advanced mode, 4× enlarging, batches, the watched folder and WebP, AVIF, TIFF and BMP.

Key facts

  • Product: EasyUpscale AI
  • Current version: 1.0.18
  • Platform: Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit); a DirectX 12 graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel); about 1 GB of disk space; 8 GB of RAM recommended
  • Price: free up to 2×, no watermark; €14.99 one-time license (Advanced mode, 4×, batches, watched folder, extra formats), no subscription
  • Languages of the app: 15 (English, Czech, German, Polish, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese)
  • Technology: models run locally through ONNX Runtime and DirectML. Real-ESRGAN (BSD-3-Clause) and SwinIR (Apache-2.0) models, plus an own text model
  • Privacy: pictures are never uploaded. Only the license key and the computer name are sent when a license is activated or checked
  • Output formats: PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, BMP
  • Website: easyupscaleai.com
