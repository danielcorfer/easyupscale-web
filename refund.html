Refund Policy – EasyUpscale AI

Try it free first

EasyUpscale AI has a free basic version with no time limit and no watermark. It enlarges images up to 2×; the license adds 4× enlarging and the other paid functions. Before buying, you can check that the app works on your computer and with your images the way you need.

Refund within 14 days

You can withdraw from the purchase within 14 days and we refund the full price, without giving a reason, for example because you changed your mind or no longer need the app.

Key for one clean result (€1.49)

The key for one clean result without a watermark in the web demo works like the license: within 14 days of purchase you can withdraw without giving a reason and we refund the full price, even if the key has already been used. After a refund the key is blocked. If the key cannot be used, write to us.

Refund later

After the 14 days we refund if:

  • The app does not work on your computer (for example it does not start or cannot process an image) and we cannot fix the problem with a fix or a new version.
  • You were charged twice by mistake for the same license.

This does not affect your statutory rights regarding defective digital content (see the License agreement, section 6).

How to request a refund

Email info@easyupscaleai.com. Include the email address you paid with and your order number, and for a later request a description of the problem. We usually reply within 2 business days. In the first 14 days we refund the full price to the original payment method with no further conditions. For a later request we first try to solve the problem and refund if we cannot.

After a refund

The license key is deactivated after the refund and the app returns to the free version (up to 2×).

Microsoft Store

The license can be bought from Polar on our website or in the Microsoft Store as an add-on of the App. The rules above apply to purchases from Polar. A purchase in the Microsoft Store is refunded by Microsoft under the Microsoft Store refund rules (https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-store); we cannot refund it ourselves.

Seller

Payments are processed by Polar, which sells the license as our Merchant of Record and issues refunds. Under its own terms (https://polar.sh/legal/terms), Polar may also issue refunds in other cases.
