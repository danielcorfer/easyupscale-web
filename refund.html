Refund Policy – EasyUpscale

Try it free first

EasyUpscale has a fully featured free version with no time limit. Every feature works without a license; the results simply carry a watermark. Before buying, you can check that the app works on your computer and with your images the way you need.

When we do not refund

A license key is digital content that is delivered and usable immediately after payment. When you buy, you expressly agree to immediate delivery and acknowledge that you thereby lose the 14-day right of withdrawal. For this reason, purchases are not refundable for a change of mind or because you no longer need the app.

When we refund

  • The app does not work on your computer (for example it does not start or cannot process an image) and we cannot fix the problem with a fix or a new version.
  • You were charged twice by mistake for the same license.

This does not affect your statutory rights regarding defective digital content (see the License agreement, section 6).

How to request a refund

Email info@easyupscale.cz within 14 days of purchase. Include the email address you paid with, your order number and a description of the problem. We usually reply within 2 business days. We will first try to solve the problem; if we cannot, we refund the full price to the original payment method.

After a refund

The license key is deactivated after the refund and the app returns to the free version with a watermark.
