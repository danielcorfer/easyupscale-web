Offline AI photo upscaler for Windows

EasyUpscale AI enlarges and sharpens photos with AI on your own PC. Nothing is uploaded, there is no subscription and no queue: your graphics card does the work.

Download free for Windows · Buy a license · €14.99

Why upscale photos offline

  • Privacy. Client shots, family photos and company images never leave your computer.
  • No recurring cost. One license, one price, no credits per picture.
  • Upscaling works without internet. Images are processed locally; a connection is only needed to activate and to check the licence at least once every 7 days.
  • No limits from a web service. No file size caps, no waiting in a queue, batches of whole folders.

What it does

  • Enlarges photos 2× and 4×, or to a target size such as 4K or Full HD.
  • Auto mode recognizes the picture (photo, drawing, text, damaged shot) and picks the model by itself.
  • Advanced mode (with the license) lets you choose from six models, including our own text model, blend two models and fine-tune sharpness and noise.
  • Before-and-after slider so you can check exactly what changed.
  • Batch processing and a watched folder that enlarges new pictures in the background.
  • Saves PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF and BMP and keeps transparency and metadata.

Good for

  • Old and low-resolution family photos
  • Scanned prints and negatives
  • Small web images that need to go to print
  • Product and real-estate photos
  • Illustrations, drawings and anime-style art

Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a DirectX 12 graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), about 1 GB of disk space and 8 GB of RAM recommended.
