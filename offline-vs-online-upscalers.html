EasyUpscale AI vs online AI upscalers

Online AI upscalers and EasyUpscale AI do the same job in different places. This page compares them honestly, including when an online tool is the better choice.

Where the work happens

  • Online upscalers: your picture is uploaded to a server, processed there and sent back.
  • EasyUpscale AI: your graphics card does the work, on your own PC. Nothing is uploaded.

Privacy

  • Online upscalers: the picture, and often your account, pass through someone else's servers. Check each service's terms for how long files are kept.
  • EasyUpscale AI: pictures never leave the computer. Only the license key and the computer name are sent when a license is checked.

Price

  • Online upscalers: typically credits per picture or a monthly subscription, often with a free tier for small files.
  • EasyUpscale AI: one payment of €14.99, no credits and no subscription. The free version enlarges up to 2× with no watermark; the license adds Advanced mode and 4×.

Limits and speed

  • Online upscalers: commonly cap the file size or resolution, and you wait for the upload, the queue and the download.
  • EasyUpscale AI: no cap from a service. Speed depends on your graphics card, and whole folders can be processed in a batch or from a watched folder.

Control

  • Online upscalers: usually one or a few presets.
  • EasyUpscale AI: Auto mode picks the model and look by itself, Advanced mode (with the license) lets you choose from six models, blend two of them, set the size and fine-tune sharpness and noise, with a before-and-after slider.

When an online tool is the better choice

  • You only need one small picture once and do not want to install anything.
  • You are not on Windows 10 or 11 with a DirectX 12 graphics card.

When EasyUpscale AI is the better choice

  • Your pictures are private, from clients or from your family.
  • You enlarge many pictures, or you do it regularly.
  • You prefer a one-time price to credits or a subscription.
