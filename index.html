Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The calculation runs in your browser on your graphics card. Only the model is downloaded from the server.

Is the demo really free?

Yes, 2× enlargement is free, the result has a sharp watermark across the whole image. A clean result without the watermark costs €1.49 per image, and a licence key removes it for good. The Windows app is free without a watermark at 2×, and the licence unlocks 4× enlargement.

Which browser works best?

In a current Chrome or Edge with WebGPU support. Without WebGPU the calculation runs on the processor and is noticeably slower.

Why is the app faster?

The app uses the graphics card directly, without browser limits. In our measurement on an RTX 4060 it was about 2–3× faster; the result differs between computers.

What if my image is large?

The app accepts a result of up to 120 Mpx. In the browser a large image can run into the memory limit, so the app suits bigger pictures.

Does it work on systems other than Windows?

The web demo does, in any browser with sufficient support. The full app is for Windows only.

What if I change my mind about the licence?

You have 14 days to get your money back.

Does the licence work without internet?

Enlarging runs offline. The licence key is activated online and the app checks it when it starts. Without internet the licence lasts 7 days, then you need to connect.

What are the requirements for the app?

Windows 10 (version 1903 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit, a graphics card with DirectX 12 support, about 1 GB of disk space and 8 GB of memory recommended.
